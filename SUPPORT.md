# Getting help

- **How do I …?** Ask in the Discussions tab of
  [deepfake-workbench](https://github.com/dfwb-research/deepfake-workbench/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open a bug report (include `dfwb doctor --json`).
- **Security issue:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never post it publicly.

These projects are maintained by one person, so replies may take a few days.
